Before you sign up

Fair questions.

How often will you email me?

Once a week. That's it. No daily sends, no surprise sequences.

Is this therapy?

No. The Weekly Wrap-Up is educational and reflective writing, not clinical care, and subscribing doesn't create a counselor–client relationship. If you'd like to work together, get in touch.

Can I unsubscribe?

Any time, in one click, from the bottom of any email. No hard feelings and no exit survey.

What do you do with my email address?

I send you the Weekly Wrap-Up. It is never sold, rented, or shared.
